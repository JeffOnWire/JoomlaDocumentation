Articles grouped by category can be quickly and easily displayed on your website. This tutorial explores two types of layouts, the Category Blog Layout, and Category List layout. The Category Blog Layout presents articles in a rich layout where each article is displayed with its title, an optional image, and article details. The Category List layout displays a simple list of article titles that link to the article. This tutorial demonstrates how to create both layout types.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.
  • 2.1 Article & Menu Item
  • 2.2 Creating a Category

Steps

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. View the frontend of the site. Find the main menu and note the menu items. We will be adding a new menu item.

Creating a Menu Item for a Category of Articles

This tutorial uses standard Joomla test data in which there are three articles in a category called Joomla. We'll be making a menu item called Joomla Article Blog and Joomla Article List that will display these articles on our website. The tutorial uses the same articles and category to create both layouts (so only one set is required).

You should choose a category and menu item names that make sense for your site. Make sure there are two or more articles in your chosen category so you can see the result, and each article should contain a couple of paragraphs of content (lorem ipsum is fine).

Create a Category Blog Layout

The Category Blog menu item creates a page that displays a list of articles in a blog type layout. Each article includes an image, article details (e.g. author name and publication date), and the text of the article.

  1. In the left side menu of the administrator homepage, click Menus > Main Menu. Result: A list of menu items is displayed.
  2. Click on the New button. Result: A new-menu-item form appears.
    • Title: a name that makes sense for your site. We chose Joomla Article Blog.
    • Menu Item Type: click Select, then in the popup expand Articles and click Category Blog.
    • Choose a Category: select the category that makes sense for your site. We chose the Joomla category.

  3. Click Save & Close. Result: The form closes and your new menu item appears in the list of main menu items.
  4. Switch to the frontend.
  5. Refresh the page then find the new link in the Main Menu. Click to follow it to view a blog layout of the articles in your chosen category. Clicking on the title of any article will take you to the full article.

Create a Category List Layout

The Category List menu item creates a page that displays a list of articles in a simple list. Clicking on the title of an article takes you to the full article.

  1. Return to the backend.
  2. In the left side menu, click Menus > Main Menu. Result: A list of menu items is displayed.
  3. Click on the New button. Result: A new-menu-item form appears.
    • Title: a name that makes sense for your site. We chose Joomla Article List.
    • Menu Item Type: click Select, then in the popup expand Articles and click Category List.
    • Choose a Category: select the category that makes sense for your site. We chose the Joomla category.

  4. Click Save & Close. Result: Your new menu item appears in the list of main menu items.
  5. Return to the frontend and refresh the page.
  6. Find the new link in the Main Menu and follow it to view a list layout of the articles in your chosen category. Clicking on the title of any article will take you to the full article.

Concepts

This tutorial demonstrates a powerful capability of Joomla. By organising your articles by category you can easily and quickly make lists of those articles available in either a blog format or as a simple list.

The nature of the menu link determined the layout; the same articles and category were used for both.
