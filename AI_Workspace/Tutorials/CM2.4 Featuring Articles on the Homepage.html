In Joomla, featured articles are those designated for special emphasis on the site, to appear more prominently than other articles. This tutorial describes how to designate a featured article that appears on the homepage.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.

Steps

The Home menu is a Featured Articles Menu Item

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. In the Administrator Menu, click Menus > Menu Items.
    • A list of menu items appears.
  3. Observe that the Home menu item is an Articles > Featured Articles menu type.

Create a Featured Article

  1. In the Administrator Menu, click Content > Articles.
  2. Click on the New button and create a new article:
    • Title: Marsupials
    • Article Text: A marsupial is a group of mammals characterised by giving birth to highly underdeveloped young, which then complete their development inside a protective external pouch on the mother's abdomen.
    • Category: Mammals
    • Featured: Yes
  3. Save & Close the article, returning to the list.
  4. Find your article in the list by filtering for featured articles.
    • Click Filter Options to display the filters, then set Select Featured to Featured Articles. Result: Your article appears in the list.
    • Click Clear to clear the filter.
  5. View the site. Result: The article appears as a featured article on the homepage.

Concepts

Featured articles appear on the homepage because the Home menu item is a Featured Articles menu type.

Featured articles may use a read more button to control the amount of article text that appears in the article list.

The article list can be filtered to find featured articles easily.

Article order is managed in the Blog Layout tab of the menu item.
