Articles are written and edited using an editor that manages the underlying HTML markup language of the web. These are often referred to as "What You See Is What You Get" (WYSIWYG) editors, and a variety are available. Joomla comes standard with a popular editor called TinyMCE. In this section we will look at how to use TinyMCE to edit an article.
