In this tutorial we will add an unordered (bulleted) and ordered (numbered) list to an article.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.

Steps

Unordered List (Bullets)

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. In the Administrator Menu, click Content > Articles.
  3. Click on the New button. Result: A new-article form appears.
  4. In the Article Text area, put your cursor at the point where you want the list to start.
  5. Type the first list item and press Enter so it becomes a paragraph. Repeat this for every item that needs to be on your list.

  6. Select the content you've just typed.

  7. In the editor toolbar, click the Unordered list icon (the one with the bullets).

  8. Result: Your unordered list is created.

Ordered List (Numbers)

In this part of the tutorial we'll add an ordered (numbered) list to our content.

  1. Create a new article, or go to Content > Articles and open the article that needs to contain the list.
  2. Put your cursor at the point where you want the list to start.
  3. Type the first list item and press Enter so it becomes a paragraph. Repeat this for every item that needs to be on your list.

  4. Select the content you've just typed.

  5. In the editor toolbar, click the Ordered list icon (the one with the numbers).

  6. Result: Your ordered list is created.

An ordered list doesn't have to use numbers; there are six options to choose from. Click the small arrow-down next to the ordered list icon to pick a different one.

Adding Sub-items to Your List

Your list can have sub-items, as in this example:

  1. In your list, select the items that need to be sub-items.

  2. Click the Increase indent icon in the editor's toolbar (or press the Tab key). Result: The items you had selected become sub-items on the second level of your list.


You can have multiple levels; every time you click the Increase indent icon, your selected list items move to the next level.

In the example we've used an unordered list, but it works the same for both ordered and unordered lists.
