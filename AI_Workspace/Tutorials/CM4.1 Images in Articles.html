This tutorial describes how to add images to articles.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.
  • We will be using this image for the tutorial. Right-click and download to your computer. 

Steps

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. Create a new article.
    • In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Articles.
    • Click on the New button. A new-article form appears.
    • Title: Astronomy
  3. Click into the Article Text area then select Media from the CMS Content menu.

    • The Media page appears in a popup frame.
  4. Click to add a new folder and name it Astronomy.
    • The page refreshes with the new folder shown.

  5. In the left-side navigation area, click on the new folder.
    • The page refreshes and displays the new folder.
  6. Add the Crab Nebula images you downloaded earlier to the new folder by drag-and-drop or using the Upload button at the top of the page.

    • The images are being uploaded to your new folder but have not yet been added to your article.
  7. Click the image to select it.
    • In the Additional Data form add descriptive alternate text, Crab Nebula
    • Click the Insert Media button at the top of the form.
    • The frame closes and the image has been added to the article.

  8. Click on the image to select it.
    • A blue bounding box appears with square handles at the corners.
  9. Drag the handles to resize the image. 
  10. Right-click over the image and select Image.
  11. Check the Show caption box then Save.

  12. The image returns with a caption area. Type a caption, The Crab Nebula.

  13. Save the article by clicking the Save button at the top of the screen, then click Preview to see how the image will appear in the article.
  14. Close the preview screen. 

Concepts 

  • Images can take a long time to download, making your page slow. Optimizing images by using appropriate size, resolution, and format can speed up page load time. 
  • Images are stored separately from articles on the server. A single image can appear in multiple articles. Deleting an article does not delete the image. 
  • Adding alternate text to an image is important for accessibility. More information can be found in this WebAIM Alternative Text article.
