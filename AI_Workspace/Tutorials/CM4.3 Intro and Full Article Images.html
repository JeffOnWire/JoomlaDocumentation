The intro image of an article can be shown on a category blog page (a page that shows articles from one or more categories).

The full article image can be shown in the detailed view of an article.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.
  • an article. See 2.1 Article & Menu Item.
  • a category blog page. See 2.3 Category Menu Item for a category of articles.

Steps

Adding Intro and Full Article Images to Your Article Content

In this tutorial we will add an intro image and a full article image to your article.

In our example, our article is called Demo article images and it's in the category Demo Stuff. This category has three articles.

The menu item is set to show one leading article and four intro articles in two columns. The direction of the columns is set to across. That means intro article #1 will be shown in the left column, intro article #2 in the right column, and if we have a third article, it will be in the left column again.

In the frontend, the page looks like this:

Our articles don't have images yet. We're going to add them now.

Intro Image

In this part of the tutorial, we'll add the intro image. This is the image shown on the category blog page.

  1. Create a new article, or go to Content > Articles and open the article that needs images.
  2. Click on the Images and Links tab.
  3. In the top left part you can add the intro image. Click on the Select button to add an image.
  4. A popup shows the images that are already there. In this case, the image we want to use isn't there yet so we will upload it. Click the Upload button in the top left corner.

  5. Upload an image from your computer. Result: The image appears in the popup, preselected, with a message confirming it's uploaded.

  6. Click Select in the top right corner. Result: The selected image is added. You can set alt text for your image; if it doesn't have to be read aloud by screen readers, check the box next to No description. Leave the other settings as they are.

  7. Save your article. Result: Our frontend page now has an image for Demo article images.

Full Article Image

In this part of the tutorial, we'll add the full article image. This is the image shown on the article page.

At this point, our article Demo article images doesn't have a full article image yet.

  1. Create a new article, or go to Content > Articles and open the article that needs images.
  2. Click on the Images and Links tab.
  3. In the bottom left part you can add the full article image. Click on the Select button to add an image.
  4. A popup shows the images that are already there. In this case, the image we want to use is there, so check the image and click Select in the top right corner.

  5. Result: The selected image is added. You can set alt text for your image; if it doesn't have to be read aloud by screen readers, check the box next to No description. Leave the other settings as they are.

  6. Save your article. Result: Our frontend page now has an image for Demo article images.
