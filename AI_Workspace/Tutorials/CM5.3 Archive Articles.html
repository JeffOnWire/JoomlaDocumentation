This article explains how to archive articles on your website, for example if the content is not very current or relevant, but you want to keep it available. In Joomla, you can simply change the status of your article to Archived.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.
  • A website with multiple articles. If you haven't created any articles yet, see 2.1 Article & Menu Item.
  • 5.1 Filter Articles

Steps

Everything we describe here takes place in Joomla's Administrator.

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. In the Administrator Menu, click Content > Articles.

Archiving an Article in Edit Mode

  1. Open the article for editing by clicking on its link in the list.
  2. Set the status to Archived.

  3. Click Save & Close. Result: Your article is archived and will no longer be visible in the default Articles list.

Finding Archived Articles

Archived articles no longer appear in the default Articles list.

  1. Click Filter Options, then select Archived from the - Select Status - filter. Result: The list of archived articles appears, each with an archive icon in front of its title.

Displaying Archived Articles on Your Website

Once articles have been archived, there are several ways to view them from the site frontend.

Via a Menu

Use the Archived Articles menu item type to link to archived articles. You can choose one or more categories to display articles from.

Via the Articles Module

Use the Articles module and set it to Archived articles only.

Un-archive Articles

To un-archive an article, the same process applies — just change the status of your article from Archived to Published.

Archiving an Article from the Articles List

  1. Check the box next to the title of the article (or articles) you want to archive. Result: The Actions button in the toolbar is enabled.
  2. Select Archive to archive the selected articles. Result: A confirmation message appears, and the archived articles disappear from the list. You can find them by filtering the list as demonstrated above.


Archiving via a Menu Item

If your article is linked in a Single Article menu item type (see 2.1 Article & Menu Item), you can archive it from there.

  1. In the Administrator Menu, click Menus, then click on the menu containing the menu item.
  2. Select the menu item to edit it.
  3. The Select Article field has an Edit option that opens a popup Edit Article form.

  4. Set the Status field to Archived (as shown in the screenshot under Archiving an Article in Edit Mode above).
  5. The menu item will still link to the archived article.

Concepts

Articles in Joomla are the main content types on a web page and can display text and images.

A menu item is a link to an article, grouped with other links in a menu.

A module is a block on your website that you can use to display content.

Archived is a status for content.

When you open an article for editing (or create a new article), you're in Edit mode.

Archived articles are filtered from view in the Articles list. You must change the Status filter to Archived to view them.

Archiving does not unpublish an article.

Archives are structured by year.

Articles can be archived by editing the article and updating its status, as demonstrated in this tutorial. It can also be done directly in the article list view. In the last tutorial, 5.2 Delete Articles, we set the article status to Trashed directly from the articles list.
