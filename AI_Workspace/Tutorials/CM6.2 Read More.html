By default, layouts such as a category blog menu item display a list of articles that include all of the text for each article. Use a Read More link to show some of the article text with a "read more" link to the full article.

Prerequisites

  • 1 Tutorial Introduction covers where to run your tutorial and administrative access.
  • 2.1 Articles & Menu Item
  • 2.2 Creating a Category
  • 2.3 Category Menu Item

Setup

Create a Category

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Categories.
  3. Click on the New button. A new-category form appears.
    1. Title: Astronomy
  4. Save & Close

Create Two Articles

  1. In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Articles.
  2. Click on the New button. A new-article form appears.
    • Title: Shooting Stars
    • Article Text: Copy and paste several paragraphs of Lorem Ipsum
    • Category: Astronomy
  3. Save & New to save and quickly add a second article.
    1. Title: Meteors
    2. Article Text: Copy and paste several paragraphs of Lorem Ipsum
    3. Category: Astronomy
  4. Save & Close

Create a Category Blog Menu Item

  1. In the Administrator Menu, expand Menus and click on Main Menu.
  2. Click on the New button—a new-menu-item form appears.
    • Title: Astronomy Blog
    • Menu Item Type: Articles > Category Blog
    • Choose a Category: Astronomy
  3. Save & Close

Steps

  1. View the site and click on Astronomy Blog in the Main Menu. The two articles appear along with the full text of each. This is not an efficient layout for multiple articles.
  2. Return to the backend. Navigate to Content > Articles and click on Shooting Stars to edit the article.
    • Place your cursor between the first and second paragraphs.
    • Select Read More from the CMS Content dropdown in the toolbar. A line of red dots appears between the paragraphs.
  3. Save & Close

  4. Repeat for the Meteors article.
  5. View the site and refresh the Astronomy Blog. The page is more manageable and articles are much easier to find.
  6. Explore the Read More links.

Concepts

  • Intro text
    The introduction text can be the first paragraph of your article, or part of the first paragraph
  • Full text
    The full article that site visitors will read.
  • Read more 
    Read More will be presented as a text link, that will show the full article.  It will be located below the intro text. You can customise the link and the link wording to anything.  It does not have to say simply "Read More".
