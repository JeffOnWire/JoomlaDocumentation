These tutorials demonstrate basic article-publishing concepts useful in managing website content.

  • 7.1 Publishing Articles and Categories — the basics of creating articles and categories and how both must be published for the article to be viewed.
  • 7.2 Publishing Menu Items — how menu items are used to view articles and lists of articles.
  • 7.3 Timed Publishing — controlling when articles are available on the website by setting start and finish times.
  • 7.4 Restrict Access to Articles — managing user roles and permissions for articles.
  • 7.5 Locking an Article — how Joomla locks an article being edited to prevent conflicting changes, and how administrators can check in an article left locked.
  • 7.6 Edit Permissions for Articles — managing the Author, Editor, and Publisher roles and the permissions that govern them.

Publishing and Access features of Joomla are powerful tools for managing site content.
