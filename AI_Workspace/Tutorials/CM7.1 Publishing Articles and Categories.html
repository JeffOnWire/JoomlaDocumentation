Setup

Create a New Category

  1. Log in to Joomla backend and navigate to the Home Dashboard.
  2. In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Categories.
  3. Click on the New button. A new-category form appears.
  4. Enter a title, Sundial Software
  5. Note that in the Status field, Published is the default status.
  6. Save & Close

Create Two Articles

  1. In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Articles.
  2. Click on the New button. A new-article form appears.
    • Title: Sun Daze Companion
    • Category: Uncategorised (the default category).
  3. Expand the save button and select Save & New. A new-article form appears.
    • Title: Olympus Solar Calculator
    • Category: Sundial Software.
    • Featured: Yes.
  4. Save & Close

Steps

Unpublishing an Article

  1. View the site by clicking the icon in the header and observe that both articles appear on the homepage as featured articles. The article details show the category—the articles are in different categories.
  2. Return to the backend. In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Articles.
  3. Click on Sun Daze Companion to edit the article.
    • Set the Status to Unpublished then Save & Close.
  4. View the site, refresh the homepage, and observe that Sun Daze Companion no longer appears. Articles must be published to appear in the website.
  5. Return to the backend and view the article list.
    • Observe that the Status icon for Sun Daze Companion is a gray X (unpublished) while the icon for Olympus Solar Companion is a green check (published).
    • Hover over the icons and observe the popup messages.
    • Click on the gray icon to publish the Sun Daze Companion
    • Articles can be published or unpublished from the article list or by editing the article.
  6. Confirm that both articles again appear on the homepage (refresh if necessary).

Unpublishing a Category

  1. Return to the backend. In the Administrator Menu, expand Content and click on Categories.
    • Observe categories have a status icon similar to the articles.
    • Hover and observe the popup message.
    • Unpublish the Sundial Software category by clicking on the icon.
  2. View the site, refresh the homepage, and observe that the Olympus Solar Calculator no longer appears.
    • An article and its category must both be published for an article to appear in the website. 
  3. In the backend, view the article list (Contents > Articles
  4. Note the status icon for the Olympus Solar Calculator is now a gray dash.
    • Hover over the icon and view the popup message.
    • In the article details below the title, observe that the category Sundial Software appears with (unpublished) indicator.

  5. Edit the article and observe Sundial Software is selected in the Category dropdown, but that it appears in brackets indicating that the category is  unpublished.
  6. Close the article without saving.
  7. Navigate back to the category list and edit Sundial Software by clicking on the title.
    • Change the Status to Published then Save & Close.
  8. View the site, refresh the homepage, and verify that both articles appear.

Concepts

  • Articles must have a category. It is a required field and it is not possible to create an article without a category. There is a default category in a fresh Joomla installation is Uncategoriesed.
  • Articles and their category must both be published for the article to appear on the website.
  • The status icon in the article and category lists indicates the publication status. Clicking the icon toggles the status.
  • When developing a new article or category it may be helpful to keep it unpublished to avoid it being displayed on the website until it is ready.
  • Unpublishing a category will prevent all articles in that category from being displayed on the website. Care must be taken when unpublishing active categories. 
  • The article list shows the category for each article, and unpublished categories are noted in this list. Articles with unpublished categories can be identified in this way. 
  • When editing an article, unpublished articles appear in brackets in the category list.
