This section introduces the essentials of Joomla and guides new users through the first steps of setting up and managing a site. It covers the basics of what Joomla is, its core features, how to get it running, and the fundamental tasks you’ll perform when creating and organizing content.

This section is designed to give you a smooth start, from installation to adding your first articles, menus, and modules, so you can quickly become familiar with Joomla’s structure and workflow.

  • Core Features – Explore Joomla’s built-in functionality, including user management, menu and content handling, multilingual support, extensions, and template customization. This helps you understand the main tools at your disposal before you start building.
