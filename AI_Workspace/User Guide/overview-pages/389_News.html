News Feeds use Really Simple Syndication (RSS) and its newer cousin Atom to share content between websites. Joomla can work with feeds in both directions: it automatically publishes your own site's content as a feed, and it can pull in and display feeds from other sites so visitors see fresh outside content without leaving your pages.

  • News Feeds – How Joomla publishes RSS and Atom feeds for your site out of the box, and how to show or hide the feed link and syndication module.
  • News Display – Three ways to bring feeds from other sites onto your pages: a sidebar module, dedicated feed pages, and category-based feed lists.
